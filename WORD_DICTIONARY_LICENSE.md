Changes by Buduj słowa: selected only words present in the course; normalized case and apostrophes; resolved inflected forms; shortened and edited meanings; added workplace and construction meanings, grammatical explanations and corrections. Entries marked `edited` have been edited or supplied in the app. General meanings are not a claim that every sense fits every sentence. Full phrase meanings come from the user's course dictionary, separately from this lexical dataset.
